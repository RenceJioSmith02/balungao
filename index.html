About Mt. Balungao

A destination where natural springs, mountain scenery, and outdoor recreation come together.

Our destination

Adventure and Relaxation in Pangasinan

Mt. Balungao Hilltop Adventure and Hot Spring Resort is located in Balungao, Pangasinan. It combines refreshing spring pools with outdoor adventure activities in a mountain setting.

Visitors can relax in hot and cold spring pools or participate in recreational activities such as zipline riding, ATV driving, hiking, and trekking.

The resort provides an experience suited for individuals, families, friends, and groups looking for both recreation and relaxation.
